# Hierarchy

Nested by `skos:broader`, starting from the top concepts. A *top concept* is the
most general concept in the scheme — the entry point for a broader/narrower
chain — and is tied to the scheme with `skos:hasTopConcept`.

A term with more than one parent is listed in full once and cross-referenced
after that. A polyhierarchy has no single correct tree, and expanding a shared
subtree under every parent would multiply the page rather than explain it.

Each top concept collapses, so this page opens as eight lines rather than as
250. Also in [Collections](../collections/), which groups the same terms without
asserting hierarchy, and in [All terms](../all/) alphabetically.
